**Payins** (collections) credit one of your books from an external payer — open banking, mobile money, cards, and other sources depending on your workspace.

## Sources

```bash
curl https://api.keette.com/v1/payins/sources \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_…"
```

Use the returned source ids / methods when building prepare and create payloads.

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview or fee">
    `POST /v1/payins/preview` and/or `POST /v1/payins/fee` to show the customer what lands on the book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare">
    `POST /v1/payins/prepare` — may return a redirect URL, payment intent, or next step for the payer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create / complete">
    `POST /v1/payins` to finalize when your flow requires it. Track with `GET /v1/payins/{id}`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Subscribe to payin / inflow webhooks so you do not rely only on redirects.

## Dev mode

In dashboard **Dev**, partner collection rails are not called. Use **Bank Simulator** to credit sandbox books and exercise balances, emails, and webhooks.
