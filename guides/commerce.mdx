**Commerce** lets a business workspace sell one-time and recurring products, bill customers, and collect through hosted checkout (crypto, NGN bank transfer, KES MoMo). Collected funds clear first, then settle into your book — available balance is **not** credited on collect.

Requires a business workspace with Commerce enabled. Money amounts are major-unit floats with 6 decimal places plus `*_minor` Ledger integers — see [Money amounts](/guides/money).

## Typical flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a product">
    [`POST /v1/products`](/guides/commerce-products) — one-time or recurring catalogue item. Or skip ahead with a one-shot checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a customer">
    [`POST /v1/customers`](/guides/commerce-customers), then attach a product or create an invoice / subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open checkout">
    [`POST /v1/checkouts`](/guides/commerce-checkout) returns a hosted `checkout_url`. Send it to the payer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify payment">
    Poll [`GET /v1/checkouts/{id}/payment`](/guides/commerce-checkout) or listen for [`event.commerce.*`](/webhooks/events#commerce) webhooks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard

In the Keette app, **Overview → Commerce** shows the same KPIs as [`GET /v1/commerce/dashboard`](/api-reference/commerce/dashboard): volume, success rate, method mix, trend, and recent collections. Use the API for your own ops dashboards; use the app for day-to-day monitoring.

## Settlement

Successful collections clear first; Keette settles them into your book automatically. Do not treat a paid checkout as spendable available balance until settled. See [Settlement](/guides/commerce-settlement).

## Webhooks

Subscribe to `event.commerce.checkout.*` and `event.commerce.invoice.*` for payment outcomes — details and examples on [Event types — Commerce](/webhooks/events#commerce).
