Successful checkouts **clear** first. Your available balance is credited only after **settlement** — an automatic Keette backoffice step. There is no public API to trigger settle.

## When it runs

Keette settles pending collections on a regular schedule (typically within the same UTC day as the successful payment). Until then, treat the collection as cleared but not yet spendable.

## How the amount is calculated

Settlement credits the workspace book you chose in Settings (**USD** or **USDC**):

| Fee setting | What you receive |
| ----------- | ---------------- |
| Fees on you (default) | Product amount minus the Commerce fee |
| `pass_fees_to_payer: true` | Full product amount (payer already covered the fee) |

Currency conversion (when the payer paid in another currency) happens at clear time into USDC clearing; settle then moves the due amount into your chosen settlement book.

## What this means for your integration

- A paid checkout / webhook success means the payer paid — **not** that funds are already in `available`.
- Use dashboard / transaction status (`pending_settlement` → `settled`) if you need to know whether settle has completed.
- Prefer webhooks for payment outcome; do not poll for settle timing in a tight loop.
